# Experience Mode: Marketing Content

Version: **3.0.0 starter profile**

Mode ID: `marketing-content`

This optional mode is inactive unless explicitly selected.

# Purpose

Convert source-supported product, service, customer, or company information into a persuasive but accurate marketing experience.

# Architecture

Recommended structure:

1. Audience and problem
2. Value proposition
3. Product or service story
4. Differentiators
5. Evidence, specifications, or proof points
6. Use cases
7. Objection handling or FAQ
8. Call to action
9. Source and legal review notes outside the public-facing experience

# Content Model

Possible outputs:

- interactive product story;
- campaign landing-page content;
- sales enablement presentation;
- service overview;
- event or launch material;
- customer-facing explainer;
- modular social or email copy package.

# Claims Policy

- Every factual, comparative, performance, compliance, warranty, availability, and customer-outcome claim must map to the source.
- Do not invent testimonials, market leadership, customer logos, benchmark results, or certifications.
- Clearly separate product facts from positioning language.
- Preserve material limitations, prerequisites, and exceptions.

# Interaction

Use interactions that improve understanding:

- use-case selector;
- feature-to-benefit reveal;
- product hotspot;
- comparison view;
- FAQ search;
- configurable CTA.

Do not add graded assessment unless explicitly requested.

# Narration

Narration and Auto Play are optional. If enabled, they use the selected UI and voice rules.

# Deliverables

1. selected marketing primary artifact;
2. message map;
3. claims/source matrix;
4. QA and legal-review notes;
5. README.

# Mode QA

Release blockers include:

- unsupported or exaggerated claims;
- invented customer evidence;
- omitted material limitations;
- inaccessible CTA or interactions;
- brand inconsistency;
- source facts altered for persuasion;
- visual design that resembles technical training when a marketing experience was selected.
